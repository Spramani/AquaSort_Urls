Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play AquaSort? +
Tap or drag a glass tube to select it, then tap another tube to pour. You can only pour liquid if the destination tube has enough space and the top liquid color matches the liquid you are pouring. Sort all colors so each tube contains a single color to win!
I got stuck. What should I do? +
If you find yourself stuck, you can:
  • Use the Undo button to step back a move.
  • Click the Hint lightbulb to see potential moves.
  • Click the Add Tube button to add a temporary empty tube to the level.
  • Restart the level at any time using the restart button in the top right.
How do I unlock new themes? +
Go to the Themes tab from the home page. You can unlock premium gradient themes using the coins you earn by completing levels, or unlock the Custom Theme creator to build your own.
How do I restore my purchases or premium subscription? +
If you reinstalled the app or changed devices, open the Shop/Subscription menu in settings and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchases are linked to your App Store account.
