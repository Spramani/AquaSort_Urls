Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to AquaSort ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

2. Information Collection & Use

AquaSort is designed to be a self-contained offline puzzle game.

  • Personal Data: We do not collect, request, or store any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • Game State Data: Game progress, coins, stars, and theme selection are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to external servers.
  • Game Center: If you sign in to Apple Game Center, leaderboards, scores, and accomplishments are shared with Apple services under your Apple account rules. We do not store this data.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

Our app offers optional digital purchases and subscriptions. All payment transactions, authentication, and subscriptions are handled securely by Apple's App Store payment processing systems. We do not collect or store your billing details, credit card numbers, or transaction information.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party analytics (like Firebase) or ad networks (like AdMob) to analyze usage patterns and show standard ads. These services collect anonymous diagnostics, installation IDs, and advertising IDs (IDFAs) only with your permission (via App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS).

5. Children's Privacy

Our game is family-friendly and is designed for all audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Support page at AquaSort Support.
